### Better write-out and a help dialog

- **Write out** now produces detailed, coherent text: related points are merged into paragraphs, and technical terms and background are explained. Specific facts such as names, dates or figures are still never invented.
- Bullet-point notes are now recognized reliably and written out on a normal click; lists of complete sentences stay lists.
- New **Help** entry in the menu next to the AI button explains all functions.
