### Conversation summaries

- **Actions → Summarize Conversation** in Issues, Opportunities, Leads and Quotations summarizes the emails and comments of the timeline for a handover: what the request is about, what has been done or agreed, and what is open.
- The summary opens in a preview, as a bullet list or as prose. **Add as Comment** posts it to the timeline, marked as an AI summary with date and model; **Copy** copies it. Nothing is saved before that.
- Quoted email history is left out. For very long timelines only the newest messages are used, and the summary says how many.
- **AI Writer Settings → Conversation Summary** sets the document types, the format, the maximum length and the prompt. All included messages are sent to the AI service.
