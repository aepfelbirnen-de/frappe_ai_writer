### Reply drafts in the email composer

- When you reply to an email, the button **Draft Reply** next to the message field writes a reply from the incoming message and the document it belongs to (title and status).
- Quoted history in the incoming message is ignored; only the new part is used.
- Optional **notes** steer the content (e.g. dates or decisions); **Regenerate** uses them. **Insert** puts the draft above your signature and the quoted message.
- The reply never promises dates, prices or deliveries that are not in your notes. Adjust the instructions under **AI Writer Settings → Reply Draft**.
