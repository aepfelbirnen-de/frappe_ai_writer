### Preview before replacing

- Results are now shown in a **preview** before they replace your text: removed words are struck through, new words are highlighted.
- **Apply** (Ctrl+Enter) writes the result into the field, **Regenerate** asks again, closing the preview (Esc) keeps your text.
- **AI Writer Settings → Preview** controls when the preview appears: for long texts (default, from 300 characters), always, or never.
