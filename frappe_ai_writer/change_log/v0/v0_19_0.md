### Cover letters for more sales documents

- Besides quotations, **Write Cover Letter** now works in drafts of **sales orders** (order confirmation), **delivery notes** and **sales invoices**, and **Write Reminder Text** in **dunnings**, with a friendly or firm tone.
- **AI Writer Settings → Cover Letter Document Types** holds one row per document type: the target field, whether amounts may be sent, and an optional purpose that replaces the default instructions.
- Prices, totals and outstanding amounts are only sent where **Send Amounts** is checked; otherwise the AI is told not to mention any.
- The button only appears in drafts for users who may edit the document. An existing quotation field is moved into the new table automatically.
