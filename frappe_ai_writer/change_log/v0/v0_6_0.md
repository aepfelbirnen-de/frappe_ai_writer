### Translate and protected text

- A new **translate** button (文A) next to the AI button translates the selection or the whole field. The menu offers the languages a manager has enabled in **AI Writer Settings → Translation Languages** (German and English by default).
- Text between `/"` and `"/` is never corrected, written out or translated, for example `/"Müller & Söhne GmbH"/`. It does not even reach the AI service, and the result is rejected if the protected text would change.
- Changes to AI Writer Settings now take effect on the next page load, without clearing the cache.
