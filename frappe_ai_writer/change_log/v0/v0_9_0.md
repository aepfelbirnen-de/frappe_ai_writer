### OpenAI, Ollama and other AI services

- **AI Writer Settings → Provider** now offers Anthropic, OpenAI, Ollama and OpenAI-compatible services such as Mistral, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, DeepSeek, LM Studio or vLLM.
- With Ollama, texts can be processed by a local model and never leave your network; no API key is needed.
- Correcting, writing out, translating, protected text and token usage work the same with every provider.
- Adjust the token prices under **Usage** when you switch to another provider.
