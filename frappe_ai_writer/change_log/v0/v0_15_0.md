### Glossary and house style

- **AI Writer Settings → Glossary and House Style** holds fixed terms for all texts.
- **Protected** terms, such as product or company names, are never changed or translated. When correcting and translating, they are replaced by placeholders before the request and do not reach the AI service; for reply drafts and cover letters, where they come from the email or the quotation, the AI is told to keep them unchanged.
- For the other terms you can add a **note** (e.g. preferred spelling, never "Offerte") and **translations**, one line per language such as `en: quotation`; they are sent only when the term occurs in the text.
- The **house style** holds short rules for all texts, e.g. "Address customers formally. No exclamation marks." It applies to corrections, translations, reply drafts and cover letters.
