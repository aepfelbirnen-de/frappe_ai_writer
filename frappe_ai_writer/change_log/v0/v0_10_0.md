### Cover letters for quotations

- In a draft quotation, **Actions → Write Cover Letter** writes the letter text from the customer, contact person and line items, and shows it in a preview to apply or regenerate.
- Prices, discounts and totals are never sent to the AI service.
- Notes already in the cover letter field are worked into the letter; text between `/"` and `"/` stays unchanged.
- Set up under **AI Writer Settings → Cover Letter (Quotation)**: choose the quotation field that holds the letter in your print format and, if needed, adjust the prompt.
