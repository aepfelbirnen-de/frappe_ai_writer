### Keyboard shortcuts

- **Ctrl+Shift+K** (⇧⌘K on macOS) corrects the field that has the cursor, exactly like a click on ✦: the selection, or the whole field without a selection.
- **Ctrl+Shift+L** (⇧⌘L) opens the menu of that field; choose a mode with the arrow keys and Enter, Escape closes it.
- They work in text editors, the comment box, the email composer and text fields, and do nothing in read-only fields or when no AI Writer field has the cursor.
- Both shortcuts are listed in Frappe's keyboard shortcut dialog (Shift+/) and in the AI Writer help.
