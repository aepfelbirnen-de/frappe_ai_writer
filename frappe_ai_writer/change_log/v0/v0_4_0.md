### Write out notes

- If a field contains only notes or bullet points, the AI button (✦) now writes them out as complete text. Normal text is corrected as before, and lists of complete sentences stay lists.
- New menu entry **Write out** turns any text into complete sentences and paragraphs.
- The written-out text uses only what the notes say: no invented names, dates or figures, and no added greeting or sign-off.
- The field and document type (e.g. *Description · Task*) are passed along so the text fits its purpose.
