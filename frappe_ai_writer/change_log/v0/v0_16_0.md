### Monthly budget

- **AI Writer Settings → Monthly Budget** caps the estimated costs per calendar month, calculated with the configured token prices. The usage summary shows a progress bar for the current month.
- At the **Warning Threshold** (default 80 %) and when the budget is used up, AI Writer managers (or System Managers if there are none) get a notification and an email, once per month. A changed budget starts the count again.
- **Block When Exceeded** refuses new requests once the budget is used up, before anything is sent to the AI service, until the next month or until the budget is raised.
- With **Track Usage per User** enabled, a **Monthly Limit per User** applies the same to each user.
