### AI correction in text fields

- The AI button (✦) now also appears in plain text fields (*Small Text*, *Text*, *Long Text*), at the bottom right of the field.
- Without a selection the whole field is corrected; with a selection only the selected part.
- The arrow next to the button offers *Correct*, *More formal* and *More casual*.
- Cmd+Z / Ctrl+Z undoes a correction.
- In the comment box the button sits at the right end of the field.
- The button sparkles while a correction is running.
